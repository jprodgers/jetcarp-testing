A-7: JETCARP 0.4.2-rc.5 in a new Windows account (the Acer)
===========================================================

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
- Do every numbered step, in order, exactly as written. Nothing is meant to be skipped.
- An indented block is commands only. Select the whole block, copy it (Ctrl+C), click in the
  PowerShell window, paste it (right-click, or Ctrl+V), and then PRESS ENTER. Pressing Enter runs
  the block's last line; without it, the last line just sits at the prompt.
- "Copy the output" means: select all the text in the PowerShell window, copy it, paste it at the
  end of your notes file, and save the notes (Ctrl+S).
- Your notes file is  C:\Users\Public\jetcarp-a7-notes.txt  (Claude reads it from your account).
  Write down anything that confuses you, even if it works out.

Part A: done.
Part B: done (the upgrade in B3 didn't happen: jetcarp.exe was in use. Part C redoes it).

Part C: the upgrade from 0.4.1, again
-------------------------------------
Ada's settings, hunt and ratings from Part B are still there, so 0.4.1 starts with them.

C1. Install 0.4.1 again, and turn on its desktop background.
  1. Close every PowerShell window (click the X at its top right).
  2. Click Start, type  PowerShell , and press Enter.
  3. Paste this block, then press Enter. It takes a minute; wait until the prompt comes back:

cd ~
scoop bucket add jetcarp https://github.com/jprodgers/scoop-jetcarp
scoop install jetcarp
jetcarp --version
jetcarp host start
jetcarp host wallpaper on
jetcarp host login on
jetcarp host status

  4. Copy the output into your notes. (Expected: "jetcarp 0.4.1" in it, and at the end the host
     running with "wallpaper: showing". If the bucket line says it already exists, that's fine.)
  5. Press Windows key + D. Write in your notes what the desktop shows: Windows' own picture, a
     JETCARP scene that is still, or a JETCARP scene that moves. Press Windows key + D again.

C2. Put rc.5's programs in place of 0.4.1's.
  1. Click in the PowerShell window. Paste this block, then press Enter:

jetcarp host stop
Get-CimInstance Win32_Process -Filter "Name like 'jetcarp%'" | Format-List ProcessId, CommandLine
Get-Process jetcarp* -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue | Stop-Process -Force
cd ~\Downloads
Copy-Item rc5\jetcarp-0.4.2-rc.5-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc\* (scoop prefix jetcarp) -Recurse -Force
jetcarp --version

  2. Copy the output into your notes. (Expected last line: jetcarp 0.4.2-rc.5. The second line
     lists any JETCARP program still running after the host stopped; the third stops it.)

C3. Sign out and in, and meet rc.5.
  1. Sign out: click Start, click  Ada Test  at the bottom left of the Start menu, click  Sign out .
  2. Sign in: on the sign-in screen click  Ada Test , type the password, press Enter.
  3. Look at the desktop. Write in your notes what you see: Windows' own picture, a JETCARP scene
     that is still, or a JETCARP scene that moves.
  4. Click Start, type  PowerShell , and press Enter. Paste this block, then press Enter:

jetcarp --version
jetcarp host status

  5. Copy the output into your notes.
  6. Paste this, then press Enter:

jetcarp

  7. The app opens. If it shows the "Photosensitive seizure warning", click
     I don't have epilepsy or a seizure disorder .
     Write in your notes what the app showed first, and what it shows next.
  8. Press Windows key + D. Write in your notes what the desktop shows now (still, or moving).
     Press Windows key + D again.
  9. Close the app: click the X at the top right of the JETCARP window.
 10. Click in the PowerShell window. Paste this block, then press Enter:

jetcarp host status
jetcarp jobs list
jetcarp jobs status 2
jetcarp library list --class spaceship
Get-CimInstance Win32_Process -Filter "Name like 'jetcarp%'" | Format-List ProcessId, CommandLine

 11. Copy the output into your notes. (Expected: the same two jobs as in Part B, rc.5's host
     running, and the app no longer in the list.)

C4. Remove it all.
  1. In the same PowerShell window, paste this block, then press Enter:

jetcarp uninstall --yes --settings --data
scoop uninstall jetcarp
scoop bucket rm jetcarp
Get-CimInstance Win32_Process -Filter "Name like 'jetcarp%'" | Format-List ProcessId, CommandLine

  2. Copy the output into your notes.
  3. Press Windows key + D. Write in your notes what the desktop shows (Windows' own picture?).
     Press Windows key + D again.

The end
-------
  1. Save your notes (Ctrl+S).
  2. Click Start, click  Ada Test , click  Switch user , and sign in to your own account.
  3. Tell Claude you're done. Don't remove Ada's account yet: Claude reads the notes first.
